Project

Curtains, sheers

From great heights under a stone vault to the windows of a village house: lined curtains, sheers and dividing curtains, calculated to the centimetre, made up in the workshop and fitted on site.

The craft

A successful curtain comes down to three things: the fullness, the drape and the finish at the bottom. The fullness is calculated according to the heading chosen, the drape depends on the lining and the weight of the fabric, and the hem is set once the curtain has been hung, when the material has finished dropping.

  • Wave, Flemish, inverted box pleat, pinch pleat or eyelet headings
  • Thermal linings, blackout linings or a simple cotton voile
  • Dividing curtains and full-height curtains for function venues
  • Matching sheers, Japanese panels and Roman blinds
  • Tracks and poles supplied, fitted and adjusted
