What you should know about our services

We offer a complete range of services to make your home more beautiful. Every project is carried out with meticulous care, so that it meets your expectations and reflects your own style.

01 — Curtains & sheers

Made-to-measure fabrics

We offer a complete range of services to make your home more beautiful. Whether it is elegant curtains, light sheers or furnishing fabrics, every project is carried out with meticulous care, so that it meets your expectations and reflects your own style.

  • Wave, Flemish, inverted box pleat or eyelet headings
  • Thermal and blackout linings
  • Sheers, Japanese panels, bed canopies
  • Roman blinds, roller blinds, pelmets
  • Poles and tracks supplied and fitted
See the “Curtains and sheers” project
02 — Cushions & seating

One-off pieces

We excel at bringing your furniture back to life — sofas and armchairs alike — using fabrics of the highest quality. Our upholstery expertise guarantees results that combine comfort and beauty while preserving the character of your pieces.

  • Full reupholstery of armchairs, sofas and benches
  • Seat cushions, back cushions and scatter cushions
  • Outdoor furniture: terraces, poolsides, pergolas
  • Made-to-measure headboards and bedspreads
  • Outdoor fabrics with UV-resistant and water-repellent treatments
See the “Cushions” project

Method

How a project unfolds

Four simple stages, from first contact to the final fitting.

Stage 01

The appointment

We come and see the space, to understand how the room is used, its light and your constraints. Measurements are taken on site.

Stage 02

Choosing the fabric

Samples, drape and finishes are shown to you. We refine the choice together until the right material is found.

Stage 03

The making

Cutting, assembly and finishing are all carried out in the workshop, on rue Villars in Nîmes.

Stage 04

The fitting

Installation on site, adjusting the drape and the pleats, checking every detail before handing the piece over to you.
