Project

Cushions

Outdoor spaces dressed from end to end: a garden lounge set, built-in stone benches and terraces. Seats, backs and scatter cushions are cut on site, then made up in the workshop in fabrics built to live outdoors.

Outdoor cushions Type of project
April 2023 Date
Gard Location
Outdoor fabrics Materials

The job

Every seat was measured to the centimetre so that it follows the structure exactly, whether in woven resin, in wood or built in stone. The foams are chosen to suit their use — firmer for the seats, softer for the backs — and sleeved in removable covers that can be washed and are treated against UV light.

  • Dimensions taken on site, with templates for the shapes that are not rectangular
  • High-resilience open-cell foam, free-draining for outdoor use
  • Removable covers with concealed zips
  • Water-repellent outdoor fabrics, UV-treated and chlorine-resistant
  • Matching scatter cushions, made from the same ranges
