Meet us

The workshop, rue Villars

A workshop in the heart of Nîmes, a sewing machine, metres of fabric and more than twenty years spent learning how a material falls.

Anne Fricher

The craft comes first

Anne Fricher guides you in creating a home that is both distinctive and comfortable, drawing on her expertise in upholstery and decoration. Whether you are after elegant wave-pleated curtains, made-to-measure blinds or a sofa brought back to life, every project is handled with care and precision.

Nothing is subcontracted: cutting, assembly and finishing are all done in the workshop. That is what makes it possible to take up a hem, adjust the fullness or remake a curtain heading without argument, until the result is right.

What matters

Three convictions

01

Measuring on site

A drawing never tells the whole story: no wall is ever straight, no window ever quite square. Measurements are always taken in your own home.

02

The right material

A beautiful fabric badly chosen will hang badly. Weight, body and weave matter as much as the pattern — we choose them together, samples in hand.

03

The finishing

The last few centimetres make all the difference: hems adjusted after fitting, pleats dressed into shape, linings that never show.

Who we work for

Private homes, hotels and restaurants

The workshop works for private homes as much as for places open to the public — hotels, restaurants, function rooms — where the demands of cleaning and of lasting well over time are that much greater.
